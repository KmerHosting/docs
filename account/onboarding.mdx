## How recommendations are interpreted

- Business websites, blogs, portfolios, and light e-commerce usually fit Shared Hosting.
- APIs, server applications, and infrastructure workloads that fit Linux containers usually fit LXC VPS Hosting.
- KVM VPS should be considered when full virtual-machine isolation is required; new KVM orders depend on current external capacity and may be marked **Out of stock**.
- Learning and test projects can start small and move to a larger service later.
- Custom requirements should be reviewed with support before ordering.
