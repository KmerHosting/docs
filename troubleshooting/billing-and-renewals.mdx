---
title: "Billing and renewal troubleshooting"
description: "Resolve KmerHosting credit, invoice, renewal, cancellation, and provisioning-state questions."
---

## Payment or credit is pending

Check the transaction in the Customer Dashboard before initiating another payment. External payment networks can confirm asynchronously. Keep the payment reference, amount, time, currency, and payment channel; never send a PIN, CVV, one-time code, or full card number.

## Auto-renew did not complete

Confirm that auto-renew was enabled before the product cutoff, the account had enough available credit, the service was eligible, and the current renewal price and billing term were accepted. Then check whether the service is active, in grace, suspended, scheduled for cancellation, or terminated.

## Purchase or provisioning failed

Do not place repeated orders until the first order state is known. A failed internal service payment restores the applicable amount to KmerHosting account credit once the failure is recorded. If the order is pending, follow its product status or retry control instead of creating a duplicate.

## Cancellation changed the service state

Some product portals schedule cancellation at period end as soon as auto-renew is disabled; others expose a separate cancellation action. Use **Keep service**, **Resume**, or **Revoke cancellation** if offered. Contact support before the displayed termination time if you need help reversing it.

Provide support with the account email, service ID, invoice or transaction reference, timestamp, displayed state, and sanitized error.
