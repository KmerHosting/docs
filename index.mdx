## Services

Service documentation covers Domains, Professional Email, cPanel and DirectAdmin Shared Hosting, LXC VPS, and management of existing KVM VPS instances. Availability, prices, and plan limits come from the live catalog. Services that are not orderable are tracked on the [roadmap](/platform/roadmap).

## Products

Product documentation is separate from service documentation. The currently documented product is [wFileManager](/products/wfilemanager), a self-hosted file manager for a Linux server you control.
