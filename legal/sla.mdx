This page summarizes availability and support principles. The canonical KmerHosting SLA & Availability policy controls where this documentation differs from it.

## Official document

See the current policy at [kmerhosting.com/sla](https://kmerhosting.com/sla).

KmerHosting does not publish one universal contractual uptime percentage, automatic service-credit schedule, or 24/7 human-response guarantee for every product. A stronger service-specific commitment applies only when it is written in the relevant order, plan, agreement, or service-specific SLA.

For current platform incidents and maintenance, use [status.kmerhosting.com](https://status.kmerhosting.com).

## Before opening an account-specific request

Prepare:

- the account email;
- the order or invoice ID when applicable;
- the affected service;
- the date, time, and timezone of the event;
- useful evidence without passwords, one-time codes, private keys, or other secrets.

<Warning>
If this documentation page differs from the official SLA & Availability policy or a service-specific written agreement, the applicable official document takes precedence.
</Warning>
