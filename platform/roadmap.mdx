---
title: "Product roadmap"
description: "Understand how KmerHosting documents planned products and availability."
---

Planned services and products are listed on the public [KmerHosting roadmap](https://kmerhosting.com/roadmap). A roadmap entry describes direction, not a launch date, reservation, price, region, SLA, or commitment to a final feature set.

This documentation gives operational procedures only for released products or the management of existing customer services. It does not mix planned products into current setup guides.

Use the [live catalog](https://kmerhosting.com/pricing) to determine what can be ordered now. Out-of-stock products can remain documented so existing customers can manage them.
