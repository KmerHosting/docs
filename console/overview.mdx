---
title: "Customer console"
description: "Use the KmerHosting customer console for account, ordering, billing, and service workflows."
---

The customer console is the source of truth for account administration, purchases, checkout, billing, and product flows that are not available through the public API. It also shows the customer-facing service inventory and links to developer tools.

Use the public API, SDK, CLI, or MCP server only for operations documented in this site. They intentionally cannot expose secret-bearing, provider-administration, or broader account-management workflows.
