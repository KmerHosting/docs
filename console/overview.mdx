The Customer Dashboard is the source of truth for central account work. It provides the overview, service inventory, live product links, developer tools, API keys, notifications, account credit, invoices and receipts, renewals, account settings, and account analytics.

Product portals hold specialized workflows:

| Portal | Typical work |
| --- | --- |
| Domains | Search, registration, transfer, contacts, DNS, security, renewal, documents |
| Email Hosting | Domain setup, DNS verification, mailboxes, webmail, limits, billing |
| Shared Hosting | Purchase, credentials, cPanel/DirectAdmin access, usage, renewals, upgrades |
| LXC | Deploy, instance controls, metrics, snapshots, terminal, web domains, billing |
| KVM | Existing instance controls, password reset, snapshots, renewals, cancellation |

Opening a product portal does not create a second KmerHosting identity. Use the same customer account and follow the ownership links shown in the dashboard.

Use the public API, SDK, CLI, or MCP server only for operations documented in this site. They intentionally cannot expose secret-bearing, provider-administration, or broader account-management workflows.

## Start your first service

After creating an account, open **Getting started** in the Customer Dashboard. Choose the goal that matches what you want to do; each card opens the existing order flow rather than creating another account:

- **Host a website** for shared hosting with a site, database, email, and SSL.
- **Use WordPress** for cPanel hosting and its WordPress installation flow.
- **Run an app or VPS** for an LXC container with root and SSH access.
- **Create business email** for an Email Hosting plan, domain connection, and mailboxes.
- **Register a domain** to search, register, transfer, and manage DNS.

You can return to **Getting started** at any time from the sidebar. Before ordering, use **Credit and payments** to confirm your available prepaid balance. Service prices and available payment options are shown in the final product order flow.

## Reading the dashboard

**Usage and spending** shows values with a stated comparison or unit: available credit compared with outstanding debt, service counts by category and status, paid receipts over time, upcoming renewals, and recorded API usage. It does not use a full progress bar to imply that account credit has a capacity or target.
