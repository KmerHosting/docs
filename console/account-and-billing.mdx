The [Customer Dashboard](https://dashboard.kmerhosting.com) is the central place for account-level work. Product portals may show service-specific invoices or renewal controls, but the dashboard gives the cross-product view.

## Account credit and purchases

KmerHosting services use the available account credit shown in the dashboard. Add credit through a payment method offered to your account, wait for the payment to be confirmed, then return to the product checkout. Never start a second payment because a first attempt is still pending; first inspect the transaction state.

At checkout, confirm the product, plan, billing period, region, control panel or operating system, support level, renewal price, and available credit. The live catalog and final order summary control. Documentation does not reproduce changeable prices.

## Invoices and receipts

Open **Invoices and receipts** to list billing documents and download the available PDF. A product portal can also expose documents specific to that product. Keep the document ID and service ID when asking billing support for help.

An invoice describes an amount due or charged; a receipt records a completed payment. Availability depends on the transaction state and product workflow.

## Renewals and auto-renew

Open **Renewals** to review eligible services, renewal dates, billing periods, prices, and auto-renew state.

- Auto-renew uses available account credit when the product attempts renewal.
- Enabling auto-renew does not guarantee renewal if the balance is insufficient or the product is no longer eligible.
- Manual renewal lets you review the current price and period before confirming.
- Disabling auto-renew can schedule some services to end at the close of the paid period.
- Product grace, suspension, restoration, and redemption rules differ. Read the relevant product guide before the due date.

## Cancellation and recovery

Customer-facing cancellation is normally scheduled for the end of the paid period rather than deleting provider resources immediately. Where the portal offers **Keep service**, **Resume**, or **Revoke cancellation**, use it before the displayed cutoff. Contact support if the control is absent; do not assume data remains recoverable after termination.

## Failed charge or provisioning

If account credit was deducted but the service payment or provisioning is recorded as failed, the applicable amount is restored to KmerHosting account credit when the failure is recorded. This is different from an external refund to a card, Mobile Money account, or bank account. See the public [refund policy](https://kmerhosting.com/refund) for eligibility and request procedures.

<Warning>
Never send a password, API key, private key, full card number, CVV, PIN, or one-time code to support. Provide only the service or transaction reference, timestamp, amount, and sanitized error.
</Warning>
