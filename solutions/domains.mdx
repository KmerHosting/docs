## Standalone domains

Standalone domain cards can show registration and renewal prices, registration and expiry dates, auto-renewal state, nameservers, transfer lock, DNS records, and renewal controls.

<Frame caption="A standalone domain includes commercial and DNS information in one service card.">
  ![A standalone domain includes commercial and DNS information in one service card.](/images/screenshots/standalone-domain-card.png)
</Frame>

## Included domains

Included domains are associated only with service records whose order or legacy entitlement explicitly includes a domain. Shared Hosting and some existing service records can have such an entitlement; LXC VPS does not include a free domain by default. The available controls focus on nameservers and DNS, and the exact entitlement is determined by the purchased service record rather than by a generic VPS label.

<Frame caption="Included domains remain linked to the hosting service that granted them.">
  ![Included domains remain linked to the hosting service that granted them.](/images/screenshots/included-domain-card.png)
</Frame>

## Supported DNS record types

The current editor recognizes `A`, `AAAA`, `CNAME`, `MX`, `TXT`, `NS`, `SRV`, and `CAA` records. Use valid values and allow for DNS propagation after changes.
