## Services are documented separately

Domains, Email Hosting, Shared Hosting, Free Hosting, LXC VPS, and existing KVM VPS instances are services. Find their ordering, lifecycle, and portal guidance under the **Services** navigation group.

The Customer Dashboard is authoritative for the services attached to your account, billing, renewals, and portal entry points. The live catalog is authoritative for availability and pricing.
