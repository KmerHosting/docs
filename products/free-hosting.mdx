Free Hosting is a `$0/month` Shared Hosting offer. Choose **cPanel** or **DirectAdmin** in the [Shared Hosting portal](https://shared.kmerhosting.com/?category=free). No payment method, KmerHosting credit, or renewal payment is required.

## Included resources

Each KmerHosting account may have **one** Free Hosting subscription:

| Resource | Included |
| --- | ---: |
| Storage | 50 MB |
| Monthly traffic | 100 MB |
| Website | 1 |
| Domain or subdomain | 1 |
| Professional email account | 1 |
| MySQL database | 1 |
| FTP account | 1 |
| Control panel | cPanel or DirectAdmin |

The live portal remains authoritative for the current plan availability and the final resource presentation. A KmerHosting subdomain can be generated automatically, or you can connect one domain you already own.

## Free while the service remains active

Free Hosting is free **as long as the service remains active**. It is not an unconditional promise that the provider account, panel capacity, or hosted data will be retained indefinitely. KmerHosting reserves the right to suspend a Free Hosting plan at any time when operationally necessary, including when available control-panel capacity is needed for paying customers. We will provide notice where practical and legally permitted.

## Inactivity warnings and cleanup

To keep the free capacity available for active users, KmerHosting records customer-side activity such as signing in to the KmerHosting account or making an authenticated Shared Hosting management request. The current automated schedule is:

| Inactivity | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 30, 45, 53 and 59 days | Warning notification and email before suspension |
| 60 days | The Free Hosting service may be suspended |
| 75 and 83 days after the last activity | Further recovery and cleanup warnings |
| 90 days | The provider hosting account and its hosted data may be removed |

KmerHosting may act earlier when panel capacity, security, abuse, legal, or other operational requirements make it necessary. The warning schedule does not create a guaranteed retention period. A suspension for inactivity does not create a charge; recovery may require contacting support or moving to a paid plan. Signing in after suspension does not automatically restore the provider account.

Before the 60-day suspension point, sign in to your KmerHosting account and review the service. Before any cleanup deadline, download site files, databases, email you need, and other backups. After provider cleanup, recovery may not be possible.

## Choosing a paid plan

An active Free Hosting service shows a path to paid plans in the Shared Hosting portal and the KmerHosting Dashboard. Paid plans provide larger resource limits and plan-specific capabilities. cPanel paid plans can include Web Terminal and REST API access; DirectAdmin paid plans expose the paid features shown by the live catalog and do not advertise Web Terminal as a DirectAdmin capability.
