Free Hosting is a `$0/month` Shared Hosting offer. Choose **cPanel** or **DirectAdmin** in the [Shared Hosting portal](https://shared.kmerhosting.com/?category=free). No payment method, KmerHosting credit, or renewal payment is required.

## Included resources

Each KmerHosting account may claim **one** Free Hosting service:

| Resource | Included |
| --- | ---: |
| Storage | 50 MB |
| Monthly traffic | 100 MB |
| Website | 1 |
| Domain or subdomain | 1 |
| Professional email account | 1 |
| MySQL database | 1 |
| FTP account | 1 |
| Control panel | cPanel or DirectAdmin |

The live portal is authoritative for current availability and the final resource presentation. A KmerHosting subdomain can be generated automatically, or you can connect a domain you already own.

## Free while active

Free Hosting remains free while the service is active. There is no recurring charge or renewal payment.

The offer is intended for active use and evaluation. Provider accounts and hosted data are not retained indefinitely when a service is abandoned. KmerHosting may also suspend free capacity for security, abuse, legal, or operational reasons. When that happens, the user-facing portal shows the applicable reason; those suspensions are separate from the inactivity lifecycle below.

## What counts as activity

The inactivity lifecycle uses customer-side KmerHosting activity, including:

- signing in to the KmerHosting account; and
- authenticated Shared Hosting management activity.

While the service is active, recent qualifying activity resets the inactivity clock.

## Inactivity lifecycle

The current automated lifecycle is:

| Inactivity | State / action |
| --- | --- |
| 14 days | First inactivity warning |
| 17 days | Additional warning |
| 19 days | Final pre-suspension warning |
| 20 days | **Suspended** — website and cPanel/DirectAdmin account become unavailable |
| 23 days | Deletion warning, 3 days after suspension |
| 27 days | Deletion warning, 7 days after suspension |
| 29 days | Final deletion warning, 9 days after suspension |
| 30 days | **Terminated** — provider account and hosting data are permanently deleted |

The lifecycle is therefore:

**Active → Suspended (20 days inactive) → Terminated (30 days inactive)**

Notifications and email warnings are sent at the configured warning checkpoints. The portal also exposes the suspension reason and the permanent-deletion deadline.

## Suspended at 20 days

When a Free Hosting service reaches 20 days without qualifying activity:

- the KmerHosting service state becomes **Suspended**;
- the cPanel or DirectAdmin provider account is suspended;
- the hosted website becomes unavailable;
- normal panel and File Manager access is no longer offered; and
- a fixed deletion deadline is set for day 30.

The KmerHosting account itself is not deleted. The customer can still sign in to KmerHosting and see the service state and reason.

A sign-in after suspension does **not** automatically reactivate the hosting account and does **not** move the deletion deadline. If recovery is needed, contact KmerHosting support before the deadline shown in the portal.

## Terminated at 30 days

If the suspended service is not recovered before the deletion deadline, it becomes **Terminated** at 30 days of inactivity.

The portal explains that the service was first suspended at day 20 and then terminated because it remained unrecovered until day 30. At termination:

- the cPanel or DirectAdmin provider account is deleted;
- hosted files, databases, email data, and other provider-side hosting data are deleted;
- panel credentials and provider access are no longer usable; and
- normal recovery should be considered unavailable.

Keep independent backups of anything important before the deletion deadline.

## One Free Hosting claim per account

The Free Hosting entitlement is a one-time claim for the KmerHosting account.

Termination for inactivity does **not** release that entitlement. After an inactivity termination, the same KmerHosting account cannot claim another Free Hosting service, but it can still order paid hosting normally.

Administrative cleanup is separate. KmerHosting may intentionally release a Free Hosting claim when performing an explicit administrative removal.

An active Free Hosting service exposes a path to paid plans in the Shared Hosting portal. Paid plans provide larger limits and plan-specific capabilities.
